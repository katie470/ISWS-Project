Our Story

The International School Wellness Survey was created by a team of educators, counselors, researchers, and administrators who understand the unique world of international schooling — from the challenges of frequent transitions to the experience of navigating cultural differences within the school community itself.

Our survey is grounded in rigorous research and adapted specifically for the international school context. It goes beyond typical wellness assessments to capture what truly matters for students — whether they are internationally mobile or local students immersed in a school culture shaped by backgrounds different from their own.

We believe every student deserves to be seen, heard, and supported.

What We Measure

Three pillars of
student wellbeing

ISWS captures a holistic picture of student experience across three evidence-based dimensions, specifically adapted for internationally mobile students and intercultural kids navigating cultural differences within their school community.

🌿

Health

Physical and mental health indicators tailored for internationally mobile students — including sleep, nutrition, stress, and activity patterns across diverse cultural contexts.

✨

Wellness

Emotional wellbeing, academic engagement, resilience, and overall life satisfaction. We go beyond surface metrics to understand how students are truly thriving.

🤝

Belonging

Connectedness to school community, peer relationships, cultural identity, and inclusion — factors especially critical for intercultural kids and globally mobile youth.

How It Works

Simple, meaningful,
actionable

From first conversation to school-wide insights — we make the process clear and straightforward for your team, wherever you are in the world.

01

Partner with Your School

Reach out to discuss how ISWS fits your school's needs. We'll customize the rollout plan, set up your school's account, and guide your team through onboarding — at no burden to your staff.

02

Students Complete the Survey

Students engage with a thoughtfully designed, age-appropriate survey that respects their time. Available in multiple languages and accessible across devices, anywhere in the world.

Your school receives clear, confidential reports highlighting key findings across health, wellness, and belonging — with practical guidance on where to focus support and resources.
