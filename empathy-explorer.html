Why Do People Use?

Drug use rarely happens for no reason. Behind every substance is a human story — pain, pressure, curiosity, survival. Understanding the why is the first step toward real empathy and real help.

💬

A note before you read

Nothing here is about excusing harm. It's about understanding people. Addiction is recognized by every major medical organization as a health condition — not a moral failure, not a choice, not a character flaw. People who struggle with substances deserve the same compassion as anyone with a chronic illness.

⚠️ This tool is for education and empathy-building only. It does not promote drug use. If you or someone you know needs support, call SAMHSA: 1-800-662-4357 (free, confidential, 24/7) or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).
