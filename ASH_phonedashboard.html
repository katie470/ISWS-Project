Insights

Key Takeaways

The most important themes from parent responses

Near-Universal Concern

86% of all parents expressed concern about their child having a smartphone, with elementary parents at 94%.

Peer Pressure Is Real

85% are also concerned about peers having smartphones, highlighting social pressure as a major driver for early adoption.

Strong Community Interest

74% of parents want to be involved in conversations with other families about smartphone policies.

All Viewpoints

Different Perspectives

Not all parents share the same view — here's what the data shows across the full range of responses

Parents Not Interested in Delaying Smartphone Use

14 of 85 parents (16.5%) said "No" to exploring ways to wait

These parents may already manage smartphone use in ways they feel work for their family, or may see other approaches as more practical.

Elementary: 1 of 16 (6.3%)
Middle School: 10 of 46 (21.7%)
Both Levels: 3 of 23 (13.0%)

Parents Not Interested in a Pledge Program

11 of 85 parents (12.9%) said "No" to a pledge like "Wait Until 8th"

Some families prefer to make individual decisions rather than participate in group commitments, or their child may already have a phone.

Elementary: 1 of 16 (6.3%)
Middle School: 5 of 46 (10.9%)
Both Levels: 5 of 23 (21.7%)

Parents Without Smartphone Concerns

9 of 85 parents (10.6%) are not concerned about their child's smartphone

These parents may have established boundaries they're satisfied with, or their child's usage patterns may not be a source of worry.

Elementary: 1 of 16 (6.3%)
Middle School: 5 of 46 (10.9%)
Both Levels: 3 of 23 (13.0%)

Undecided Parents

7–16 parents were "Undecided" across various questions

A notable group of parents are still weighing their options. This is particularly common on the pledge question (16 undecided) and community involvement (14 undecided).

Middle school parents make up the largest undecided group
Pledge & involvement see the most indecision
These figures represent the full spectrum of parent views at ASH. Every family navigates smartphone decisions differently based on their child's age, maturity, and circumstances.
