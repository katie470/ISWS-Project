- **Base** (e.g., `YNQ_answers[0]`) — All students, count & percent

## 11. Report Section Structure

1. **Cover Page** — School, date_today
2. **Table of Contents**
3. **Introduction & Methodology** — School, date_survey, N, prettygrades, removed_N
4. **Executive Summary** — Key findings bullets, top-line statistics
5. **Key Data Analysis: Alcohol Use** — Charts, MTF comparisons, by-grade tables
6. **Key Data Analysis: Cannabis Use** — Charts, MTF comparisons
7. **Key Data Analysis: Vaping/E-Cigarettes** — Charts, MTF comparisons
8. **MTF National Benchmark Comparisons** — 14 MTF comparison tables (by grade × timeline)
9. **MTF by Gender** — 3 gender comparison tables (8th, 10th, 12th)
10. **School Climate & Environment** — 9 agree/disagree items, charts
11. **Perceived Norms** — Perception vs reality tables
12. **Higher-Risk Drinkers Analysis** — 23 consequence bullets, risk comparisons
13. **Attitudes & Policies** — Attitude tables, policy perception
14. **School Connectedness** — 8 connectedness items
15. **Family Factors** — Parental monitoring, family meals
16. **Peer Factors** — Friend problems, negative consequences from others
17. **K10 Mental Health** — 3 charts, substance use correlation tables
18. **Stress & Coping** — Stressor tables, coping substance tables
19. **Appendix A** — Full counts & percentages (all questions)
20. **Appendix B** — All questions by grade level
21. **Appendix C** — All questions by drinking risk category
